## Digital Flagship Experiences

1. **Cinematic Opening Sequence (`CinematicIntro`)**: Hairline gold animation, VN Monogram reveal, letterspaced typography, curtain dismissal with reduced-motion support.
2. **The Hero Campaign (`HeroSection`)**: Full-viewport chiaroscuro flacon imagery with interactive 3D mouse parallax tilt, gold dust particles, and dual CTAs.
3. **The Maison Manifesto (`MaisonManifesto`)**: High-contrast Soft Ivory editorial layout with macro dark orchid photography.
4. **The Fragrance Collection (`FragranceShowcase`)**: Interactive flacon switcher highlighting all four Opus creations, note pyramid preview, and detail views.
5. **The Scent Experience (`ScentExperiencePyramid`)**: Interactive 3-stage temporal pyramid (Top, Heart, Base notes) with botanical ingredients and volatility curves.
6. **The Art of Composition (`ArtOfComposition`)**: 3-step creative journey (01 Inspiration, 02 Composition, 03 Signature).
7. **The World of Velours Noir (`WorldOfVeloursNoir`)**: Edge-to-edge campaign film stills and private salon invitation.
8. **The Maison Story (`MaisonStory`)**: The three pillars of craftsmanship: The Nose & Formulation, Master Glasswork, and 6-Month Maceration.
9. **Notes From Our World (`JournalPreview`)**: Editorial magazine previews linking directly to long-form reading experiences.
10. **Final Brand Experience (`FinalBrandExperience`)**: Unforgettable closing statement with invitation protocol.
11. **Luxury Navigation & Footer (`LuxuryHeader`, `LuxuryFooter`)**: Monogram badge, sticky translucent plum backdrop blur, full-screen mobile menu drawer, custom luxury cursor, and optional generative Web Audio atelier atmosphere synthesizer.
12. **Interactive Scent Profiler (`ScentProfilerModal`)**: 3-question diagnostic protocol matching clients to their soul fragrance.

## Technical Stack

- **Framework:** Next.js (App Router, Turbopack)
- **Language:** TypeScript
- **Styling:** Tailwind CSS v4 with bespoke luxury design tokens
- **Typography:** Cormorant Garamond (Haute display serif) & Manrope (Grotesk sans-serif)
- **Motion & Interactions:** Lenis smooth scrolling, canvas gold particle physics, interactive audio synthesizer, custom magnetic cursor
- **Icons:** Lucide React
